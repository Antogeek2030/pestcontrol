Mosquito season in the Victoria area can feel endless once the heat and humidity arrive. A few consistent habits make a noticeable difference.

## Empty standing water

Check bird baths, plant saucers, clogged gutters, toys and any low spots after rain. Mosquitoes can complete their life cycle in less than a week in small amounts of water.

## Maintain the yard

Trim tall grass and dense shrubs where adults rest during the day. Keep the lawn mowed and reduce heavy ground cover near the house.

## Consider professional barrier treatments

Residual products applied to vegetation reduce the number of adult mosquitoes resting on your property. Combined with source reduction, many homeowners notice fewer bites within a short time.

## Protect evenings outdoors

Fans, appropriate clothing and EPA-registered repellents help when you are outside during peak feeding times around dusk and dawn.
