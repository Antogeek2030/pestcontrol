Ants in the kitchen are one of the most common calls we get in Victoria. Warm weather and easy access to food and water make counters and cabinets attractive.

## Start with sanitation

Wipe counters after meals, store sweets and pet food in sealed containers, and empty trash regularly. Even small crumbs are enough to keep scouts returning.

## Find the entry points

Ants often come in under doors, through window frames, or along plumbing penetrations. A bead of caulk or a door sweep can block many routes.

## Don’t just spray the ones you see

Over-the-counter sprays kill visible workers but rarely reach the colony. They can also scatter ants into new areas. Bait stations or professional treatments are usually more effective for lasting results.

## When to call a pro

If trails keep returning after you’ve cleaned and sealed, or if you see large numbers or multiple species, a licensed technician can identify the ants and treat the nest source. Same-day service is often available in the Victoria area.
